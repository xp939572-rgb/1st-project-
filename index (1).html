About

The advocate behind the case.

A client-focused approach to Civil, Family and Criminal matters — grounded in preparation and clear communication.

Saba Muzaffer Kayani is a practicing Advocate focused on providing strategic, client-centered legal representation across Civil, Family and Criminal Law.

Her practice covers property and possession disputes, recovery and injunction matters, contracts and specific performance, alongside family matters including divorce, khula, maintenance, custody and guardianship, and criminal matters spanning FIR-related proceedings, bail, and criminal applications.

Clients based outside Pakistan are supported through dedicated remote consultation services for Pakistan-related legal matters — from document review to litigation strategy.
